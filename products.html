Artist-designed T-shirt $22

Stars I Can Follow T-shirt

A soft cotton shirt featuring a night-sky drawing created during a student and community art session. Each star represents a goal the artist hopes to reach.

Sample story: Mei's night sky

Mei enjoys drawing the sky from her bedroom window. She chose five stars for five things she values: family, music, learning, friendship, and courage. Students helped prepare the final print while keeping her original lines and color choices.

Herbal tea gift pack $16

Quiet Garden Tea Pack

A selection of calming herbal tea sachets wrapped in nature patterns inspired by leaves, flowers, and peaceful afternoons.

Sample story: Arun's quiet garden

Arun finds busy rooms tiring and prefers the garden behind his community center. He photographed leaves and selected the colors for this pack. His design invites people to slow down and notice small details in nature.

Handmade textile $18

Every Color Belongs Pouch

A useful zip pouch sewn from donated fabric pieces selected and arranged during a collaborative weekend workshop.

Sample story: Sofia's color map

Sofia organizes colors by the feelings they give her. Green is restful, yellow is curious, and coral is brave. Her patchwork pattern shows how different feelings can belong in one day.
