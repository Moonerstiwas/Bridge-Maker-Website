About us

Students building connections through respectful action.

Bridge Makers is a student-led club connecting schools, NGO partners, and disability communities through creative projects, stories, and shared experiences.

Why we began

Bridge Makers began by connecting ICAN with our school community.

We are students at SWIS (Shenwai International School), an international IB school in Shenzhen, China. Our founding team first learned about ICAN, an organization that supports teenagers with intellectual and developmental disabilities as they build practical, physical, and creative skills. While completing our Grade 8 community project, we saw an opportunity to bring ICAN and SWIS closer together so both communities could learn from and better understand one another. We began by inviting ICAN participants to perform and host a student-supported charity booth at our annual Winter Celebration, a public school event joined by students, parents, teachers, media representatives, and visitors from the wider community.

How we work

Respect comes before visibility.

Listen first

Community members guide how their work, stories, and identities are represented.

Ask permission

Stories, photographs, and personal information are shared only with informed consent.

Create together

Students participate as collaborators, not as people arriving with all the answers.

Build continuity

Projects aim for lasting relationships rather than one-time appearances.
