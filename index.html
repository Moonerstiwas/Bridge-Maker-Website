Our Mission

We bring students, NGO partners, and local communities together to support disability communities.

Bridge Makers supports disabled communities by helping more people understand their stories, talents, needs, and voices. We connect students, NGO partners, and local communities through creative projects, volunteering, interviews, and handmade products so the backgrounds, stories, and lived experiences of people with disabilities can be recognized with dignity, respect, and care.
