Workshops & volunteering

Students can help through real collaboration.

This page organizes the different ways students, schools, and NGO partners can work with disability communities through creative action.

Workshop

Creative making sessions

Students and community members create textiles, cards, bracelets, paintings, or small handmade goods together. The focus is shared creativity, not rushing production.

Workshop

Product photography

Volunteers photograph handmade products, write clear descriptions, and prepare online shop materials so every item feels respectful and professional.

Workshop

Story interview days

Students practice listening, interviewing, and story writing while learning about the backgrounds and experiences of people with disabilities.

Community

Different partner groups

Projects can be designed for schools, disability service centers, family organizations, art groups, rehabilitation communities, or NGO partner events.

Inter-school network

Bring Bridge Makers to your school.

Bridge Makers is not limited to one campus. Students, clubs, and teachers from other schools can join shared projects, exchange ideas, and help organize activities with NGO and community partners.

Student teams

Start a Bridge Makers school team

Gather interested students, choose a teacher adviser, and join online planning meetings. Each team can help with interviews, workshops, accessibility awareness, storytelling, or events.

School collaboration

Host a shared project or event

Schools can work together on creative workshops, awareness weeks, volunteer visits, fundraising events, and exhibitions developed with the guidance and consent of community partners.

Join a project

Tell us how you want to help.

You can volunteer in workshops, help with interviews, support product photography, connect Bridge Makers with an NGO partner, or invite students and teachers from another school.
