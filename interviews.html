Story interviews

Real lives are understood by listening.

People with disabilities can share their daily lives, challenges, recovery journeys, interests, and ideas in their own words. Students listen respectfully and help make these experiences more visible.

Interview guide

Questions we ask

What is an ordinary day like for you? What brings you joy? What challenges do you wish others understood? What support or changes would make your community more accessible?

Student role

Listen, record, respect

Students help prepare questions, write descriptions, take photos, and edit stories only after participants and partner organizations approve the final version.

Story archive

Backgrounds that stay visible

Interviews can become a story archive that helps people understand disability communities through everyday experiences, dignity, creativity, and personal voice.

Different communities

Stories can come from many experiences.

Interview projects can focus on physical disabilities, intellectual disabilities, neurodiverse communities, chronic illness, caregivers, families, artists, or NGO staff. The goal is always to let people describe themselves instead of being defined by others.
